Homehub — one scan indicator (E2 mock)

You reported that the reading-manual bar at the top of the item page doesn’t match the design we agreed on, which was one indicator at the bottom of every page. In this version the bottom pill is that one indicator, everywhere, including the item whose manual is being read. On that item’s page, the reading state moves into the Upkeep card, where the tasks will appear. The top card goes back to its one job, handing you the review when the read is done, whether or not the manual had maintenance in it. Two honesty fixes ride along. The review’s count includes only tasks that really appear in Tasks, and a bell is never drawn when it cannot ring.

Vocabulary

The app says both “scanning” (the pill’s “1 scanning”, “Scanning the document end to end…”, and the rule in src/lib/scanCopy.ts that says never “read”) and “reading” (the design A line, the Upkeep placeholder, “We finished reading”). This mock uses “Reading” everywhere, so the pill says “1 reading”. Adopting it means retiring the “never read” rule in scanCopy.ts in the same change.

How the mock was made

Sizes, colours, radii and copy are lifted from the code, and checked against the journey screenshots 2188-J3-item-detail and 2189-J3-review-one-screen (390 × 844) and the HH-161 report shot. The status bar and home indicator are decoration. The rail and the pill’s spinner move; both hold still under reduced motion. S3, S3b and S3c use the Sharp microwave from the emulator seed, the app’s no-maintenance case. The yellow note in S4 marks rows skipped to fit the frame; it is not part of the app.
