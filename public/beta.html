What Homehub does

A schedule that builds itself

Common upkeep for the things most homes have, from your first minute. Add a manual and Homehub reads it for the exact schedule, with proactive reminders so nothing slips.

  • Start without a manual, with smoke alarms, air filters and the water heater.
  • Each task is marked Essential, Recommended or Optional, so safety jobs come first.
  • You get reminders only for the tasks you choose.
  • From a manual, Homehub sorts what it read into maintenance, cleaning, usage and setup. Nothing is saved until you press Save.

Answers about your home

Why it’s beeping, which filter it takes, how to reset it after a power cut. Ask about anything you own.

  • With the item’s manual, the answer shows the section and page it came from.
  • Without one, you get general guidance.
  • Narrow a question to one room or one item.

Know what’s still under warranty

One list of what’s covered and what runs out soon. Each item shows the date its coverage ends.

Every appliance, organized

Model, purchase date, warranty and manual for every appliance, kept in one place.

  • House notes for shutoffs, breakers and paint colors.
  • Spare parts, and where you keep them.

Beta FAQ

This is version 1, so some things will be rough. If anything is confusing, broken or missing, tell me. That’s what the beta is for.

How do I install it?

  1. On your iPhone, open the invite link.
  2. If you don’t have TestFlight yet, tap View in App Store and install it, then open the invite link again.
  3. Tap Accept, then Install.
  4. Open Homehub and enter your zip.
Join the Beta

What is TestFlight?

Apple’s free app for trying apps before they’re in the App Store. Homehub installs and updates through it, like any other app.

Why does TestFlight say "Chief Home Officer"?

That’s Homehub’s old working name, still on Apple’s invite page. It’s the same app, and it shows up as Homehub on your phone.

How do I send feedback?

Take a screenshot while you’re in Homehub, then tap Share Beta Feedback. You can also open TestFlight, pick Homehub and tap Send Beta Feedback.

Or just email chiefhomeofficerapp@gmail.com.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The beta is free, with no ads.

What do I need?

An iPhone on iOS 15 or later. There’s no Android version yet.

How many people can join?

The link has room for 50 testers. If it says the beta is full, email me and I’ll make room.

Who sees my home’s details?

Only you and anyone you invite to your home. Read the privacy policy. To delete your account, email me and it’s gone.

How do updates work?

New versions arrive in TestFlight. Each version works for 90 days, so if Homehub stops opening, check TestFlight for an update.
