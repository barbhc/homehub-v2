Homehub

Privacy Policy

Effective 10 August 2026

Homehub keeps track of the appliances and equipment in your home, reads their manuals, and works out what maintenance they need. This page explains exactly what it stores, what leaves your device, and who else sees it.

Who is responsible for your data

Homehub is responsible for the personal data described here. You can reach us at chiefhomeofficerapp@gmail.com.

What Homehub stores

  • Your account — your email address and an account identifier. If you sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only ever receive Apple's private relay address.
  • Your home — a name you choose and a time zone. Homehub does not ask for or store your street address, and it does not collect your location.
  • Your items — brand, model, serial number, purchase date, warranty details, category, room, tags, and any photos you add.
  • Manuals — PDFs you upload or links you supply, plus the text extracted from them.
  • Your maintenance plan — tasks, schedules, what you have marked done, and any corrections you make to how a task was categorized.
  • Questions you ask — the questions you put to the in-app assistant and the answers it gives.
  • Notification token — if you turn on reminders, a device token used solely to deliver them.
  • Usage data — which screens you open and which core actions you take.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use your data to build advertising profiles or to track you across other apps and websites.
  • We do not collect your location, and we do not access your contacts, calendar, or health data.
  • We do not record your screen or your voice.

Where your data goes

Homehub relies on a small number of outside services to work. Each one receives only what that specific feature needs. They process it on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Google FirebaseYour account, home, items, manuals, tasks, photos, and notification token.Sign-in, the database your home lives in, file storage, and reminder delivery.
AnthropicText extracted from manuals you add, and questions you ask the assistant.Reading a manual into a maintenance plan, and answering questions about your items.
Google Cloud VisionPhotos you take of an appliance nameplate or a receipt.Reading the text off the label so brand and model fill in automatically.
Brave SearchBrand and model names, and the wording of a question when you ask for a web search.Finding the right owner's manual, and answering questions your manuals do not cover.
IcecatBrand, model, or barcode you enter.Identifying a product so its details fill in automatically.
U.S. Consumer Product Safety CommissionBrand and model names.Checking whether an item you own has been recalled.
PostHogProduct usage events and your account identifier.Understanding which parts of the app work and which are confusing. Session recording is off.

These services operate in the United States. If you use Homehub from elsewhere, your data is transferred and processed there.

Photos and manuals

Photos you attach to an item are stored with that item and are not used for anything else. A photo you take specifically to scan a label is additionally sent to Google Cloud Vision so the text can be read back; it is used for that scan and to fill in the item's details. Manuals you add are stored and their text is sent to Anthropic to produce the maintenance tasks for that item.

Sharing a home with other people

If you invite someone to your home, they can see and edit the items, manuals, and tasks in it. Invite only people you want to have that access. You can remove someone at any time, which ends their access going forward.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete an item, a home, or your account, we remove it from the live app and delete it from our systems within 30 days. Backups are cycled out within 30 days. Error and usage records are kept for 12 months.

Your choices

  • See and correct — every item, task, and detail Homehub holds about your home is visible in the app and editable there.
  • Export — email us and we will send you a copy of your data.
  • Delete — you can delete individual items in the app. To delete your whole account and everything in it, email us and we will action it within 30 days.
  • Reminders — turn notifications off in your device settings at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to or restrict how we use your data, or to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Write to us and we will help.

Security

Your data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored by Google Firebase with access rules that limit each home's data to its own members. No system is perfect, and we do not claim otherwise; if we ever become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will tell you.

Children

Homehub is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that materially affects you, we will let you know in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version.

Contact

Questions, requests, or complaints: chiefhomeofficerapp@gmail.com.
